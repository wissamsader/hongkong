The eatery小店故事

A neighbourhood pasta and burger joint街坊的意粉漢堡小店

Neighbourhood pasta and burger joint is what HoHon Eatery calls itself, and the description fits: a teal shopfront at the Shek Tong Tsui end of Queen's Road West, three minutes from the HKU exit, where the signature is a charcoal bun beef burger under Thai green curry sauce, backed by rigatoni with homemade sausage in creamy tomato sauce, roasted cauliflower, spicy beef and Thai burgers, with a jasmine tea gin cocktail on the drinks list.「街坊意粉漢堡小店」是 HoHon Eatery 對自己的稱呼，名副其實：皇后大道西近石塘咀的一面藍綠色門面，距港大站出口步行約三分鐘。招牌是泰式青咖喱醬炭黑包牛肉漢堡，還有自家製豬肉腸忌廉蕃茄汁粗管通粉、烤椰菜花、香辣牛肉與泰式漢堡，酒單上更有一杯茉莉花茶氈酒雞尾酒。

Lunch runs 12:00 to 16:00, dinner 18:00 to 21:30, and on Mondays the room rests. There are seats inside and out, the team fronting the counter is known for steering visitors to the right plate, the space takes private dinners and catering sets, and the fastest way to reach them is the one their own Instagram gives out: a WhatsApp message.午市 12:00 至 16:00，晚市 18:00 至 21:30，逢星期一休息。店內店外均設座位，店員以樂於為客人推介菜式聞名，小店亦接受私人包場及外賣到會套餐。想聯絡他們，最快的方法正是他們 Instagram 上寫明的那一個：WhatsApp 短訊。

In the room堂食

Dine-in is the pasta and burger list proper: the charcoal bun beef burger with Thai green curry sauce, Thai burgers, spicy beef, crab rigatoni, grilled halibut, roasted cauliflower, and a jasmine tea gin cocktail from the drinks list. Prices at the counter, seats inside and out.堂食是意粉與漢堡的正場：泰式青咖喱醬炭黑包牛肉漢堡、泰式漢堡、香辣牛肉、蟹肉粗管通粉、香煎比目魚、烤椰菜花，酒單上還有茉莉花茶氈酒。價錢請到店查詢，店內店外均設座位。
