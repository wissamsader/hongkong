Our story我們的故事

The corner room街角小店

CORKS is the neighbourhood wine den of Chris and Steph, two founders from France who now call Hong Kong home. They took a corner room on Hollywood Road, folded the glass front open to the street, and let the evening run on two things: good bottles, and no hurry.CORKS 是 Chris 和 Steph 的街坊酒館。兩位創辦人來自法國，如今以香港為家。他們在荷李活道租下一間街角小室，把玻璃摺門向街敞開，讓一個晚上只講兩件事：好酒，和不趕時間。

The list leans home, from grower Champagne to easy reds by the glass, and the chalkboard does the cooking: saucisson, rillettes, cheeses cut at the counter. Happy hour is generous, the service charge does not exist. Come before dinner, stay past it.酒單帶著法國家鄉味，由小農香檳到易入口的杯裝紅酒；廚房則交給黑板：風乾腸、熟肉醬、吧檯即切芝士。Happy hour 夠慷慨，服務費不存在。晚飯前來，坐到晚飯後。
